Tower of Hanoi

Instrucions

Your goal is to move the stacked blocks to the other end. Each stack is a different colour. To move a block just click inside the coloured area and then click which stack you want it to go to. If you make a mistake or want to cancel your selection just click off of the game area. Try to beat the clock and your friends by making the least amount of moves.

Choose the number of blocks (Max 20)
